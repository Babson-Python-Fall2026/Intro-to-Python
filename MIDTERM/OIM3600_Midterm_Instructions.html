Midterm Exam Instructions

Follow these instructions in order.

Before Starting the Exam

  1. Open VS Code. Make sure all of your work has been committed and synced. Check the Source Control panel on the left and confirm there are no numbered change indicators showing pending changes.
  2. Run the update script: ..\update50.ps1
  3. Put your laptop in Airplane mode.
    Exam rules: You may use any materials already available in your repository and your own notebooks. You may not access the internet or use AI during the exam.
  4. Open the midterm notebook. In VS Code, navigate to student_repo/midterm and open OIM3600_midterm.ipynb. You have until 12:00 noon to complete the exam.

After Completing the Exam

  1. Turn off Airplane mode. Verify that your laptop has reconnected to the internet.
  2. Save and submit your work.
    • Save the completed OIM3600_midterm.ipynb notebook.
    • Commit and sync your repository in VS Code.
    • Open GitHub and verify that your updated midterm notebook appears in your repository.

Final Submission Checklist

  • Completed notebook saved
  • Airplane mode turned off and internet connected
  • Changes committed and synced
  • Updated OIM3600_midterm.ipynb confirmed on GitHub
